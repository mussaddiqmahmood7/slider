## Studies: productivity, security, and learning are different outcomes

- [Perry et al., Do Users Write More Insecure Code with AI Assistants?](https://arxiv.org/html/2211.03622v3), 2022 preprint / 2023 CCS work: original methods/results inspected. 47 participants, five security tasks across Python/JavaScript/C, older `codex-davinci-002` assistant, human ratings on matched conditions. AI-assisted participants produced less secure solutions in this setting and had greater confidence. Not a universal current-model result or real-world incident count.
- Contrasting evidence: [Sandoval et al., Lost at C, USENIX Security 2023](https://www.usenix.org/system/files/usenixsecurity23-sandoval.pdf), original paper inspected. 58 student programmers, linked-list C task, `code-cushman-001`, study-specific bug/non-inferiority analysis. It reports a small security impact under its particular margin/task. Different tasks/tools/measures prevent a pooled ratio or a claim of consensus that AI always worsens security.
- [Shen & Tamkin, How AI Impacts Skill Formation, 2026 v2 preprint](https://arxiv.org/html/2601.20245v2), §§4–6: original design/results inspected. Main analysis: 52 people with prior Python experience learning Trio, randomized GPT-4o chat assistance versus no assistance, immediate AI-free 27-point quiz of concepts/debugging/code reading. Lower average quiz performance with AI; no conclusive task-time gain. Do not repeat the paper's rounded “17%” as a precise percentage-point transformation: its displayed 4.15/27 is not that exact rounding. No learning number is displayed. This is immediate comprehension, not long-term retention or a beginner web curriculum. Interaction-pattern clusters are observational and small, not causal proof that a particular prompting style works.
- [METR February 24, 2026 update](https://metr.org/blog/2026-02-24-uplift-update/): original methods/limits inspected. Follow-up recruitment, task selection, pay changes and concurrent-agent time measurement complicate productivity estimates. Do not reuse the earlier slowdown as a universal present-day effect. No productivity multiplier displayed.
- [DORA 2025 report entry](https://dora.dev/research/2025/dora-report/) and [WEF Future of Jobs 2025](https://www.weforum.org/publications/the-future-of-jobs-report-2025/) are optional context: organizational research and employer expectations respectively. Official entries/summary inspected; no numerical result from an unread full DORA report used, and no employer forecast is treated as realized employment. Newer editions were searched/checked where needed for displayed claims, rather than assuming 2025 is latest.

No defensible global AI-caused versus human-caused security-incident count or directly comparable “blind acceptance” rate was established. Suggestion acceptance, human review, trust and non-use are not interchangeable. No “52% blindly accept”, CodeRabbit/Veracode ratio, invented learning speed, or guaranteed job timeline is used.
